# Sinch get started: Gemini app

> Host steps for the Gemini app at gemini.google.com and on phones. For Gemini CLI in a terminal, use `gemini-cli.md`. Facts come from Google's help page on custom apps (https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17209137). Follow the rules in [chat-apps.md](chat-apps.md); this file adds the clicks.

## Step 3: the Sinch docs (optional)

Google calls this a **custom app**. It is only available to people who are 18 or older, in the US, signed in with a personal Google account (not a work or school account), with **Keep Activity** on, and using Gemini in English. If any of these does not fit, skip this step without explaining the list; most work users will skip it.

It must be added on gemini.google.com in a computer browser. After that it also works in the phone app.

1. Open gemini.google.com, click **Settings** at the bottom, then **Connected Apps**.
2. Click **Add a custom app**.
3. Paste `https://developers.sinch.com/mcp` as the MCP server URL, click **Next**, and follow the prompts. No sign-in is needed.
